# Disrupt — Website Context & Brand Voice

## What This File Is

This is the source-of-truth context file for building the Disrupt website. Use it to inform all copy, tone, structural, and design decisions. When writing any text for the site, refer to the brand voice section first. When building any page, refer to the site structure and design direction sections.

## About Disrupt

Disrupt is Northeastern University's largest and oldest fintech club. We don't just talk about fintech, we build things. Our members consult for real startups, build quantitative models, publish research, and launch their own ventures through an 8-week accelerator. Every branch produces tangible work product.

**Tagline:** Where fintech gets built.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Confident, clear, direct. We sound like smart people who build things, not like a club trying to impress you with buzzwords.

**Do:**

- Lead with what we actually produce and deliver
- Use plain, specific language ("we consult for Series C startups" not "we empower students with real-world consulting exposure")
- Be concrete about outcomes, clients, and projects
- Write the way you'd describe Disrupt to a sharp person at a career fair
- Keep sentences short and punchy where possible

**Don't:**

- Use corporate filler ("empowering the next generation," "fostering innovation," "bridging the gap")
- Use em dashes
- Hedge or be vague about what we do
- Sound like a university brochure
- Group things in threes for rhetorical effect
- Use unnecessary quotes or hollow transition phrases
- Oversell, let the work speak for itself

**Voice test:** If a sentence could describe any club on campus, rewrite it until it could only describe Disrupt.

## Site Structure

### Navigation

Top nav bar, clean and horizontal: **Home | Consulting | Quant | Research | Finnovate | Events | About**

Logo on the left, nav links in the center or right, "Join" or "Apply" as a CTA button on the far right (when recruitment is open, this links to applications; when closed, it links to an interest form or disappears).

### Design Direction

**Layout reference:** The Confluence conference site (bold hero with large tagline, clean top nav with pill-style buttons, card-based content sections, strong photography, clear visual hierarchy). Adapt that structure but make it denser and more information-forward than a conference landing page. Disrupt's strength is the breadth and quality of what the club actually does, so the site should communicate that efficiently without stretching thin content across excessive scroll depth.

**Color:** The current Disrupt logo uses blue and green. The site should use a dark, premium palette (navy or near-black backgrounds with a bright accent color) that feels fintech-appropriate. Avoid the washed-out blurry dark aesthetic of the old site. Strong contrast, clean type, sharp photography.

**Typography:** Bold, confident display font for headings. Clean sans-serif for body text. Large type sizes for hero taglines and section headers. Don't use generic system fonts.

**Photography:** Use sharp, well-lit photos from actual Disrupt events where available. No blurry background images. If good photos aren't available for a section, use clean graphic design (color blocks, subtle patterns) instead of bad photos.

**General principles:**

- Every page should be scannable. Someone should get the gist in 5 seconds.
- Lead every page with what the branch/program produces, not what members "learn" or "gain."
- CTAs should be contextual: "Apply to Consulting" on the Consulting page, "Register for Finhacks" on the Events page, etc.
- Mobile-first. Most students will hit this site from their phone via an Instagram link.

## Page-by-Page Content Guide

### Home

The home page is the front door. Most visitors are prospective members who clicked a link from Instagram or heard about Disrupt at a fair. They need to understand what Disrupt is and why it's worth joining within a few seconds.

**Hero section:**

- Disrupt logo
- Tagline: "Where fintech gets built."
- One-liner: "Northeastern's largest student-run fintech club."
- Primary CTA: Join/Apply button

**Branches overview section:**

- Four cards (Consulting, Quant, Research, Finnovate), each with a 2-3 sentence description focused on output
- Each card links to its dedicated page
- Consulting's card should feature client names and valuations since those are the strongest credibility signals

**Finhacks callout:**

- A visually distinct section (not just another card) highlighting the flagship 48-hour hackathon
- Links to the Events page

**Community section (brief):**

- One short block: socials, monthly hackathons, the Disrupt Expo, partner organizations
- This doesn't need a lot of space, just enough to show the club has a life beyond the work

**Footer:**

- Apply/Join CTA
- Instagram, LinkedIn, email links
- No newsletter signup unless it's actively maintained

## Administrative Notes (not for site copy)

- **Operations** is listed as a team on the Apply page but does not have its own branch page.
- **Outreach branch** handles marketing, event logistics, and speaker sourcing. This is an internal operations team.
- **Tech team** has been dissolved and reorganized into other branches.
- The site features four main branches: Consulting, Quant, Research, and Finnovate.
- Finhacks is featured prominently on the Events page, not buried as a sub-item.
- The old tagline ("When inspiration strikes, disrupt") and old copy ("where curiosity meets innovation," "recognized the disruptive influence of technology in finance") are retired. Do not use them.
- The old site had a sidebar nav and separate pages for Home, About, Finnovate, Consulting, Events, and Newsroom. The new site replaces this with a horizontal top nav and adds dedicated pages for Quant, Research, and Finnovate.
